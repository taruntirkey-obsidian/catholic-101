# Catholic Worship: Sacrifice, Latreuo, and Honor to Saints

## TL;DR
In Catholic theology, the Mass is `latreuo` — the one sacrificial worship owed to God alone.
Honoring saints is *dulia* and honoring Mary is *hyperdulia*. These are veneration, not worship.
The Church distinguishes sharply between *worship* and *honor* using the same Greek terms the Bible uses.

---

## 1. The Mass as Sacrificial Worship - Latreuo

### Core Catholic Teaching
The Catholic Mass is not "just a memorial service". It is the **re-presentation of the one sacrifice of Christ on Calvary**.

**Key points:**
1. **Sacrificial**: Jesus offered Himself once on the Cross. The Mass makes that same sacrifice present. Hebrews 7:27, 9:24-26
2. **Latreuo**: This is *latreuo* λατρεύω — sacred, priestly, cultic worship. It is offered to God alone.
3. **Eucharistic**: Under the appearances of bread and wine, Christ is truly present: Body, Blood, Soul, and Divinity. This is why Catholics "worship" the Eucharist in Adoration.

**Catechism of the Catholic Church 1330**:
"The memorial of the Lord's Passover. The Eucharist is also a sacrifice, because it re-presents [makes present] the sacrifice of the cross."

**Matthew 4:10**: "You shall worship *proskuneo* the Lord your God and serve *latreuo* Him only" — This is exactly what happens at Mass.

So: *Latreuo* = offered only to the Triune God in the Mass and Eucharistic Adoration.

---

## 2. Honoring Saints: Dulia and Hyperdulia

Catholicism uses 3 Greek-based terms to avoid confusion. This distinction is ancient, from the Church Fathers.

| Term | Greek Root | Meaning | Given To | Type |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Latreuo** | λατρεύω | Adoration, sacrificial worship | **God alone** - Father, Son, Holy Spirit | Worship |
| **Dulia** | δουλεία | Veneration, honor, respect | **All the Saints** | Honor |
| **Hyperdulia** | ὑπερδουλία | Highest veneration | **Mary, Mother of God** | Highest Honor, but not worship |

### A. Dulia - Veneration of Saints
**What it is**: Asking saints to pray for us, celebrating their feast days, honoring their example.
**What it is NOT**: Offering them sacrifice, praying to them *as if* they are God, or giving them divine attributes.
**Biblical basis**:
- Asking for intercession: James 5:16 "pray for one another"
- Honoring the righteous: Hebrews 12:1 "cloud of witnesses", Revelation 5:8 "saints' prayers"

Example: Lighting a candle at St. Joseph's statue = *dulia*. Asking "St. Anthony, pray for me" = *dulia*.

### B. Hyperdulia - Highest Veneration for Mary
**What it is**: Mary receives more honor than any other creature because she is the Mother of God *Theotokos*.
**Why "hyper"**: Because of her unique role in salvation history. Luke 1:28 "Hail, full of grace". Luke 1:48 "All generations will call me blessed".
**What it is NOT**: *Latreuo*. Catholics do not offer Mass to Mary. We do not sacrifice to her. We honor her and ask her intercession.

**Council of Nicaea II 787 AD**: Distinguished worship due to God from veneration given to saints and images.

### C. Latreuo - Worship Due to God Alone
This is reserved for God. This includes:
1. The Sacrifice of the Mass
2. Eucharistic Adoration / Benediction
3. Prayers of adoration: "Glory Be", "Holy Holy"

If anyone offered *latreuo* to a saint or Mary, that would be idolatry according to Catholic teaching.

---

## 3. How This Plays Out in Catholic Practice

| Action | Catholic Term | Who Receives It |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Mass / Eucharist** | *Latreuo* | God Alone |
| **Eucharistic Adoration** | *Latreuo* | God Alone |
| **Novena to St. Jude** | *Dulia* | Saint Jude |
| **Rosary, Marian Feasts** | *Hyperdulia* | Blessed Virgin Mary |
| **Bowing before a Bishop** | Civil *proskuneo/shachah* | Human authority, not worship |

### Common Objection: "Catholics worship Mary and Saints"
**Catholic Response**: No. We give *latreuo* to God only. We give *dulia/hyperdulia* to saints.
The same way the Bible shows *shachah/proskuneo* to kings and *latreuo* to God only. The posture may look similar, but the intent and theological category are different.

**Example from Scripture**: Revelation 19:10 John falls to *proskuneo* the angel. Angel says "Worship God".
Catholics agree. That’s why we don’t offer Mass to saints. We ask them to join us in offering *latreuo* to God.

---

## 4. Key Takeaways
1. **Mass = Sacrifice = Latreuo**: The central act of Catholic worship, given to God alone.
2. **Saints = Dulia**: Honor and asking for prayers. Not worship.
3. **Mary = Hyperdulia**: The highest honor given to any creature, but still not worship.
4. **Biblical Language**: The Church uses the same distinction found in Greek: `proskuneo` can vary, but `latreuo` is for God.

This protects the First Commandment: "You shall have no other gods before me."

## TL;DR
In Hebrew and Greek, the main words for "worship" mean *to bow down, prostrate, or do obeisance*.
The same posture was used for God and for people in authority.
The Bible distinguishes between *civil honor* and *sacred worship*. Only God receives `latreuo` — temple/sacrificial service.

---

## 1. Core Words in Original Languages

| Word | Language | Literal Meaning | Used For |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Shachah** שָׁחָה | Hebrew | To bow low, prostrate, fall on face | God, kings, family, prophets, foreigners |
| **Proskuneo** προσκυνέω | Greek | To kiss toward, fall prostrate in homage | God, masters, kings, angels, believers |
| **Latreuo** λατρεύω | Greek | Sacred service, temple worship, cultic ministry | **God alone** |

> Because these describe posture, English translations vary wording by context.

---

## 2. Shachah in the Old Testament

### Examples of Non-Divine Use
- **To Kings**: 2 Samuel 1:2 - messenger to David. 1 Kings 1:16 - Bathsheba to Solomon
- **To Family**: Genesis 33:3 - Jacob bows 7 times to Esau
- **To Prophets**: 1 Kings 18:7 - Obadiah to Elijah. 2 Kings 2:15 - sons of prophets to Elisha
- **To Foreigners**: Genesis 23:7 - Abraham to Hittites

### Key Verses
**1 Chronicles 29:20**
`wayyiqqəḏū` "they bowed heads" + `wayyištaḥăwū` "they prostrated *shachah*" before YHWH **and** the king.
Same physical act, two recipients.

**Joshua 7:6**
Joshua "fell to the earth upon his face *shachah*" before the Ark in mourning.

**2 Chronicles 29:28-30**
Corporate religious adoration *shachah* during Hezekiah’s temple service.

---

## 3. Proskuneo in the New Testament

### Examples of Non-Divine Use
- **To Masters**: Matthew 18:26 - servant to master in parable
- **To Believers**: Revelation 3:9 - enemies will `proskuneo` at feet of Philadelphia church
- **To Jesus in Mockery**: Mark 15:19 - soldiers kneel to mock Him as king

### Instances of Attempted Worship of Humans/Angels
1. **Cornelius and Peter**
   Acts 10:25-26 — "When Peter entered, Cornelius met him and fell down at his feet and *proskuneo*. But Peter lifted him up, saying, 'Stand up; I too am a man.'"
2. **The crowd and Paul/Barnabas at Lystra**
   Acts 14:11-15 — After healing, the crowd wanted to offer sacrifices to them as gods. Paul tore his clothes: "We are men of like nature with you."
3. **John and the Angel**
   Revelation 19:10 — "I fell down to *proskuneo* at the feet of the angel... but he said, 'You must not do that!... Worship God.'"
   Revelation 22:8-9 — Same incident repeated.

> In every case, the human or angel immediately refuses and redirects worship to God.

---

## 4. Latreuo: Sacred Service Reserved for God

- **Meaning**: Temple service, priestly ministry, sacrificial worship. Not just bowing.
- **Matthew 4:10**: Jesus to Satan: "You shall *proskuneo* the Lord your God and *latreuo* Him only."
- **Romans 12:1**: "Present your bodies... which is your *latreuo*."
- **Hebrews 9:1, 6**: Used for Levitical priests serving in the tabernacle.

**Rule**: `Proskuneo/Shachah` can be horizontal or vertical. `Latreuo` is vertical only.

---

## 5. Translation Comparison

### 1 Chronicles 29:20 — "...before the LORD and the king"

| Translation | Rendering |
| --- | --- |
| **KJV** | "...and worshipped the LORD, and the king." |
| **NRSV** | "...and bowed down and did obeisance to the LORD and to the king." |
| **ESV** | "...and bowed their heads and paid homage to the LORD and to the king." |
| **NIV** | "...and they bowed down and fell prostrate before the LORD and the king." |

### General Pattern

| Context | KJV | NRSV | ESV | NIV |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **To God** | worship | worship | worship | worship |
| **To King/Person** | worship, bow down | bow down, do obeisance | pay homage, bow down | bow down, pay homage |

> KJV reflects 1611 English where "worship" = "worth-ship". Modern versions use "bow/homage" to avoid implying divinity.

---

## 6. Lawful vs. Unlawful Bowing

| Type | Example | Biblical Stance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Civil Homage** | `shachah/proskuneo` to kings, parents, prophets | **Lawful** - Cultural honor |
| **Idolatry** | `shachah` to Baal, Asherah, gold idols | **Forbidden** - 1st & 2nd Commandment |
| **Demonic Worship** | Revelation 9:20 | **Forbidden** |
| **Beast Worship** | Revelation 13:4 | **Forbidden** |
| **Angelic Adoration** | Revelation 19:10, 22:8 | **Forbidden** - "Worship God" |
| **Cultic Service** | `latreuo` to God alone | **Required** - Matthew 4:10 |

---

## 7. Key Takeaways
1. **Posture ≠ Worship**. It’s about *who* and *why*.
2. **Honor to people** = lawful cultural respect
3. **Sacred worship `latreuo`** = God alone
4. **Attempts to worship humans/angels** are always rejected in Scripture

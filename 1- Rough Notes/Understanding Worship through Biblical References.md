# Understanding Worship in the Bible: Shachah, Proskuneo, Latreuo

## 1. Big Picture
The Bible uses 3 main words for "worship". English often translates all 3 as "worship", but the original languages make important distinctions.

## 2. Shachah in the Old Testament
**Hebrew**: שָׁחָה *shachah*  
**Meaning**: To bow down, prostrate oneself, pay homage. Physical posture of humility.

### Uses:
- **To God**: Ex 20:5, Deut 6:13, Ps 95:6 "Oh come, let us worship *shachah* and bow down"
- **To People**: Gen 33:3 Jacob *shachah* to Esau 7 times; 1 Sam 25:23 Abigail *shachah* to David
- **To Kings/Angels**: 1 Chr 29:20 "bowed down to the LORD and to the king"; Dan 2:46 Nebuchadnezzar to Daniel

### Key Hebrew Examples
| Hebrew | Transliteration | Root | Meaning | Reference |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| וַיִּקְדּוּ | **wayyiqqəḏū** | q-d-h קדה | "and they bowed down" | Gen 24:26, 52 |
| וַיִּשְׁתַּחֲווּ | **wayyištaḥăwū** | sh-ch-h שחה | "and they prostrated / worshipped" | Gen 24:26, 48, 52 |

> **Note**: Gen 24:26 uses both together: "He *bowed down* and *worshipped* the LORD." Vertical `shachah`.

## 3. Proskuneo in the New Testament
**Greek**: προσκυνέω *proskuneo*  
**Meaning**: To kiss toward, to bow down, to prostrate. The NT equivalent of `shachah`.

### Uses:
- **To God/Jesus**: Matt 2:11 Magi *proskuneo* Jesus; John 4:24 "worship *proskuneo* in spirit and truth"; Rev 22:9 "Worship *proskuneo* God"
- **To People**: Matt 18:26 servant *proskuneo* to master; Matt 18:29 fellow servant
- **Wrong Target**: Rev 19:10, 22:8-9 John tries to *proskuneo* angel. Angel says: "Worship *proskuneo* God"

## 4. Latreuo: Sacred Service Reserved for God
**Greek**: λατρεύω *latreuo*  
**Meaning**: Temple service, priestly ministry, cultic/sacrificial worship. Not just bowing.

### Uses:
- **To God Only**: Matt 4:10 "You shall worship *proskuneo* the Lord your God and serve *latreuo* Him only" - quoting Deut 6:13
- **Priestly Service**: Heb 9:1, 6, 9 "regulations for worship *latreia*"; Rom 12:1 "your spiritual worship *latreia*"
- **Never used for people in the Bible**

> [!important] The Key Rule
> **Proskuneo/Shachah can be horizontal or vertical. Latreuo is vertical only.**
>
> **Horizontal** = toward people. Respect. Ex: Gen 33:3, Matt 18:26  
> **Vertical** = toward God. Worship + Sacrifice. Ex: John 4:24, Matt 4:10
>
> `shachah` and `proskuneo` describe a *posture*.  
> `latreuo` describes *sacrificial temple service* reserved for God.

## 5. Summary Table
